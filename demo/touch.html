floaty

Touch or pen, or the buttons: attachInput({ touch }). Pinch a floating window with two fingers to resize it, swipe left or right on the tab strip to switch tabs, two-finger swipe to switch workspace, swipe a monocle (one window at a time) to show the next window, long-press a window for its menu, and drag a title bar to move it (a tiled one after a short hold). A mouse never triggers these.
