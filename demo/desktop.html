Drag & drop config.drag

Drag a tiled window's title bar onto another: the center swaps, an edge inserts before/after (BSP: splits). Hold Shift to float it at the pointer; Shift-drag a floating window onto a tiled one to tile it there; drop on a workspace tab to move it. Touch: long-press the title bar. Esc cancels; one drag = one undo step.

Docking tree (the "dock" workspace, or "⇥ To docking tree"): drag a window's edge onto another to split (row/column); drag onto its center to add it as a tab; drag a tab within a group's strip to reorder or insert at that index; Shift-drag out to undock as floating (window/detach) — same gestures, interpreted by DROPS.tree.

Snap zones & magnetism config.snap

Drag a floating window's title bar near the stage's edges or corners: the overlay previews a half, quarter or maximize placement; release to apply it (one undo step). Away from the edges, moving or resizing a floating window snaps its edges onto other windows' edges and the stage within the magnet distance.
