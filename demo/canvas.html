A spatial notebook: the panes live in a world the page pans and zooms with transform: translate() scale(). Scroll the wheel over empty space (or hold Ctrl/⌘ anywhere) to zoom around the cursor, drag empty space to pan. Dragging a title bar or a grip tracks the cursor at every zoom, because attachInput and createDomRenderer get coordinates: { toStage, scale }; config.bounds: "none" turns off snap zones and clamping, so panes go anywhere, negative coordinates included.
